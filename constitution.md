# Tiny Notes constitution

Starter draft from the product brief. The product council adds and changes articles by pull request.

- **TNOTE-1** Every goal in roadmap/goals.yaml is measured as its brief says, and the measure is reported after each release that moves it. (Policy)
- **TNOTE-2** WCAG 2.1 AA (via VX-9): the page must be keyboard-operable, labelled for screen readers and meet contrast requirements, verified by automated tests. (Policy)
- **TNOTE-3** Personal-data law of the user's country (for example GDPR if they are in the EU): notes and the sign-in account are the user's personal data. They must be stored in an allowed region and be exportable and erasable on request. This must be rechecked once "where" is confirmed. No sector regulation (health, payments) applies to general-class notes. (Policy)
